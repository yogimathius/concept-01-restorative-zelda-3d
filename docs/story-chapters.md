# The Broken Waterway — playable story

For the proposed deeper narrative arc, character motivations, Stillfall continuity
and possible relationship to our universe, see the
[story and universe plan](story-universe-plan.md). Its future chapters and cosmology
are planning options, not playable or approved canon.

The current chapters include the Force/Anchor progression slice described in
[waykeeper-slice.md](waykeeper-slice.md). The broader
[canonical RPG revision](rpg-systems-restoration-revision.md) governs future traditions
and specializations. Both chapters, earlier quest completion and earned saves are
preserved while restoration interactions gain more depth.

## Premise

You are a returning waykeeper. The Stillfall storm silenced the waterway's
living channels. Mara, your old ford caretaker, recognizes your river-mark.
People have lost more than crossings: letters, food gardens, family contact,
and the bells that tell distant villages someone is still there.

## Chapter 1 — A way home

Mara gives context and two cups of Bloom Tea. Mend the first grove. Iona, an old
bridgekeeper on its far bank, teaches Force: a wounded spring cannot recover around
obstructing stone. Clear the spring and Mend it, then return for Anchor, the Warden
tradition of giving unstable systems ground to hold. Players can encounter partially
restorable willows before taking that lesson and return with new understanding.
The fractured spillway combines clearing, grounding and Mend. Behavioural gear and
mastery offer alternate ways to resolve its physical conditions.
Oren, a ferryman waiting by the spillway, can carry you into the marsh once
the route is flowing. Optional groves retain their loot and remain revisitable.

## Chapter 2 — When the marsh sings

Neri, the marsh gardener, has not heard from her brother Lio since the storm.
Her kitchen garden, the reed nursery, and the sluice bell carry the main quest.
Restore them in any order. Oren recognizes the bell's returned tone and finds
a message lodged in the reopened channel: Lio is alive in Counterweight Orchard.
He closed the upper sluice to protect the reservoir, promising to explain why.

Daro, a reed herbalist, offers an optional spring restoration and teaches
supply-based brewing. Anchor steadies the optional willow refuge; Force clears Daro's spring; the
lantern beds need stone clearing and grounding. Restoration adds supplies and
brings light back to the settlement. The kitchen garden keeps simple Mend; Force opens the nursery feed and Anchor
steadies the bell before Mend can finish. Neri and Oren offer missing techniques
through local story lessons. Anchor can later secure garden irrigation, bringing
Neri back to tending it and adding a Flow recovery place. NPCs remember that work.
See [Phase C details](journey-rebalance.md).

Completion grants the Messenger's brooch once. The next chapter is named and
framed as a future destination; Counterweight Orchard is not playable yet.

The [Reedglass correspondence route](marsh-correspondence.md) gives the bell's
letter a physical place and a short follow-through. Oren goes to the bell when
the main channel recovers. After hearing from Lio, Neri prepares a reply and
seed from her changed garden. The player carries it to Oren, who returns to the
landing with it. This one-time quest pays modest XP and scrip, persists, and
leaves the reservoir question open.

## Interaction rules

Touch TALK appears near a character. Conversation pauses movement and releases
held Mend. Short pages and two clear choices replace exposition dumps. Restore
places before or after conversation; both paths resolve correctly. Travel is an
explicit choice. Returning to the ford preserves every restored place and outfit.

Bloom Tea: +1.5 Mend reach for 30 active-play seconds.
Reed Cordial: +20% walking speed for 30 active-play seconds.
Effects do not stack with themselves; paused menus do not consume duration.
The pack shows current counts, lets the player use a cup, and Daro can brew more.
