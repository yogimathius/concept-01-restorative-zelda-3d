# Channel: Bryn's carried current

Status: **first playable Channel route**, 2026-09-27. This is an authored safe Charge circuit in Reedglass, not the complete Channel discipline or a general electrical simulation.

Once the sluice bell (M3) and lantern beds (M6) are restored, Bryn offers a Channel lesson through touch dialogue. It unlocks and equips **Carry**. The player taps the existing technique medallion near the bell capacitor, spending 12 Flow to hold a visible Charge. A later tap near an unlit ceramic receiver delivers it. There is no timer, fine targeting, third resource bar or extra permanent action button. The source and two receivers have generous ranges; they are spaced so the player must actually carry the Charge away from the source. Empty casts and repeated deliveries spend no Flow and earn no mastery.

The first receiver lights the restored living lantern beds and leaves their ecology flourishing. Later care at M6 yields one renewable lantern seed per completed care cycle, under the existing active-play cooldown. The second receiver powers Sela's exchange lamp. Each unique delivery awards 20 Waykeeper XP and three Channel mastery, and visibly changes the post. Sela recognizes that she can shape glass after sunset; Bryn moves her work toward the lit lamp. The Waykeeper Journal tracks the source, each dark receiver and the return visit. Reporting back to Bryn yields 20 XP, eight river scrip and two riverglass once. Her existing care commission remains available through dialogue.

The route uses existing save schema 5: the two persistent outputs live in M6 ecology `charged` and the `exchange_powered` story flag; `skills.channel_carry` preserves the held Charge across travel/reload. Older saves start with the circuit dark, and existing restorations remain valid. Channel mastery is meaningful only for this first slice; Reservoir, Overflow, Conductive Glide, Overcurrent, additional receivers and deeper tree choices remain to build.
