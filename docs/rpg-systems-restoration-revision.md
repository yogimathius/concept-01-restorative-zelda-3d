# The Broken Waterway — RPG Systems & Restoration Progression Revision

**Canonical builder specification. User-adopted 2026-09-27.**
This supersedes the earlier "add more restoration skills" direction in
`waykeeper-progression-direction.md`. Preserve the working implementation; this
specification defines its next evolution. A requirement being adopted does not mean
it has been implemented. See `rpg-foundation.md` and `roadmap.md` for delivered status.
The user's full specification in this conversation remains authoritative; the
sections below preserve its requirements, examples and phased execution plan.

## Existing foundation to preserve

Touch-first 3D restorative action-RPG about becoming a Waykeeper. Broken Ford and
Reedglass Marsh; 12 sites; NPC dialogue; Mend/Force/Anchor/Glide; future definitions
for Weave/Channel/Bloom; Waykeeper XP; individual mastery; six behavioural upgrades;
visible equipment/inventory; consumables; persistent multi-region saves; partial
restoration; touch controls; Warden/Weaver art/identities; Android builds. Adoption
baseline: 143 passing automated tests.

Core fantasy: explore → understand what is wrong → use learned techniques → restore
relationships/systems → visibly change the world → grow and specialize → revisit
with new knowledge → reconnect communities.

## 1. Core principles

Preserve touch-first play, generous targeting, partial recovery, clear audiovisual
feedback, meaningful visible equipment, inhabited regions, persistent saves,
readable world rules, river-order visual identity and progression independent of
conventional enemy killing. Mend is fundamental within an expanding vocabulary;
walk/hold-Mend/repeat must not dominate. Difficulty comes from understanding and
choosing techniques, not precision timing, intricate block puzzles, creature
placement, unclear input, obscure rules or punishing failure.

## 2. Stability replaces hearts

Remove the five-heart presentation from the main RPG. Stability is the HP-equivalent:
physical/spiritual grounding, represented by a polished readable bar and numbers
such as Stability 82/100. Currents, Charge, environmental impacts, unstable creatures,
collapse, overload, major falls and hostile phenomena may reduce it. Loss is not
ordinary combat damage. At zero the Waykeeper becomes **Unmoored**, recovers at an
appropriate safe/restored checkpoint and retains progress, with minimal frustration
and a gentle tone.

## 3. Flow powers techniques

Flow is the MP-equivalent. Mend, Force, Anchor, Weave, Channel, Bloom and advanced
variants consume Flow. Glide may stay free/inexpensive. Natural regeneration and
restored water, healthy springs, completion, gear, nodes, consumables and world
interactions can restore Flow. Encourage thoughtful use without early-game waiting
for repeated resource refills.

## 4. Weight, Charge and resources

Preserve Weight/Charge as conditions interacting with the two main resources.
Charge may enhance movement/Glide/Channel and Flow generation. **Overcurrent** may
allow Flow beyond its normal maximum or stronger techniques at Stability risk.
Weight may improve displacement resistance, Force/Anchor and Stability protection,
while reducing mobility and regeneration. Avoid additional permanent resource bars.

## 5. Three connected progression layers

**Waykeeper Level:** broad growth. First-time restoration, story milestones,
major discoveries, optional restoration, quests and important exploration award XP.
Leveling can grant Skill Points, Stability/Flow capacity, loadout capacity and broad
features; it must not automatically upgrade every technique.

**Skill Points:** spent by the player on chosen tree nodes. Sufficient mastery must
not automatically purchase every available upgrade.

**Technique Mastery:** actual experience with Mend/Force/Anchor/Weave/Channel/Bloom
and future disciplines. It gates eligibility for deeper nodes, alongside points and
other prerequisites. Meaningful unique interactions, restoration, quests/discoveries
award it. Prevent empty-cast, resolved-target, spam and save/reload farming.

## 6. Connected tree

One connected Waykeeper tree supports Fundamentals/Attunement, Mend/Movement,
Warden (Force/Anchor/Weight), Weaver (Weave/Channel/Charge), Living Arts (Bloom),
hybrid builds and cross-discipline intersections. It need not use one exact layout.

## 7. Fundamentals

General nodes can develop maximum Stability, Unmoored recovery, environmental
resistance and brief post-restoration protection; Flow capacity, restored-water
regeneration, completion refunds, healthy-waterway regeneration and controlled
Overcurrent. Glide examples: **Current Rider** extends travel with current;
**Flowstep** refunds Flow through restorative fields; **Wake** creates a utility wave.
Keep movement responsive and touch-friendly.

## 8. Mend tree

**Restore** remains the initial hold ability. Behavioural nodes: **Living Pulse**
spreads completion healing; **Resonance** strengthens/extends it; **Memory** restores
eligible structures toward a stable configuration; **Deep Restoration** completes
severe recovery after underlying conditions are resolved; **Gentle Current** lowers
Flow cost near healthy water. Numerical improvements are secondary to capabilities.

## 9. Warden branch

A tradition of stability, structure, Weight, Force, grounding and protecting systems.
It is not a conventional tank class. Preserve/reuse current behaviour.

Force: **Pulse**, **Draw** (pull), **Groundwave** (connected propagation), **Momentum**
(transfer movement), **Undertow** (also stabilize). Anchor: **Ground**, **Shared
Ground**, **Root Memory**, **Deep Anchor** (gain Weight), **Sanctuary** (stability field).
Deep interactions: **Foundation** makes deeply anchored restoration stability partly
persistent; **Counterweight** transfers Weight through connected systems.

## 10. Weaver branch

A tradition of connection, transfer, Charge, relationships and living networks,
not a conventional mage. Weave is the next major technique **after the benchmark**.

Weave: **Link** connects compatible entities; **Transfer**, **Equalize**, **Triad**,
**Network**, **Echo** expand property transfer, balancing, target count and linked
restoration. Channel builds on Charge: **Carry**, **Reservoir**, **Overflow**,
**Conductive Glide**, **Overcurrent** safely redirect/retain energy, route excess and
allow controlled Flow excess at Stability risk.

## 11. Bloom / Living Arts

Develop viable ecosystems into flourishing systems, usually after restoration.
**Awaken**, **Root**, **Reedway**, **Canopy**, **Symbiosis**, **Flourish** affect growth,
stability, traversal, ecological recovery, settlements/resources, wildlife, hidden
routes and future Weave interactions. Bloom must have real world utility.

## 12. Cross-discipline nodes

Provide desirable interactions at intersections: **Counterweight** moves Weight over
Weave; **Living Network** carries links through bloomed vegetation; **Grounded Current**
increases anchored Charge capacity; **Resonant Foundation** propagates Force through
linked structures; **Restorative Overflow** sends excess completion Flow to a linked
target. These should make players want to plan toward a build.

## 13. Node design rule

Minor stats are allowed (+8 Stability, +10 Flow, +8% reach, +10% Anchor duration,
+5% movement). At least every third or fourth meaningful node must change behaviour
or unlock an interaction. Avoid percentage-dominated trees; expand vocabulary.

## 14. Hero identities

The player is fundamentally a Waykeeper. Evolve Warden/Weaver toward schools,
traditions, disciplines, specializations and visual identities. Allow learning both
with distinctive deep builds. Preserve the character work; investigate outfit
traditions, initial affinity and later specialization visuals.

## 15. Restoration stages

Support damaged, exposed, viable, stabilized, reconnected, restored and flourishing
where relevant, without requiring every stage everywhere. Stone blocking a spring
can need Force; instability permits only partial Mend until Anchor; severed sources
need Weave; Bloom can develop restored systems into flourishing ones. Persist
partial stages and conditions.

## 16. World language rather than puzzle rooms

Use consistent physical/ecological relationships rather than arbitrary switch gates.
Ability gating is appropriate when the reason is visible. Remember/mark unfinished
sites and create natural return opportunities. Keep interactions readable/forgiving.

## 17. Reedglass progression

Preserve straightforward Mend moments, but Chapter 2 should not become mechanically
simpler than Ford. Evolve main-story sites with learned techniques and deeper outcomes:
Neri's garden can have an Anchor/Bloom extension; nursery obstruction can use Force;
the bell can have stabilization/Weave states. Preserve saves and approachability.

## 18. Gear builds

Preserve physical replacement in world and animated Gear preview. Reach/rate/speed
become secondary statistics. Expand behavioural pieces and real trade-offs, following
Ferryman's staff and Stonekeeper brooch. Examples: **Riverweave Mantle** retains Charge
through watery Glide; **Echo Thread** propagates Mend over links; **Foundation Torque**
strengthens Force against heavy objects at extra Flow cost; **Quietwater Cloak** improves
restored-water regeneration but dissipates Charge; **Keeper's Weight** trades mobility
for maximum Stability. Gear should define a Waykeeper build.

## 19. Gear/tree interactions

Gear may temporarily grant access, boost effective rank, alter cost/behaviour, expose
a hybrid early or strengthen one branch while weakening another. A relic could pass
Weight through one link before Counterweight is learned. Do not invalidate tree
investment; support build possibilities.

## 20. Consumables

Preserve Bloom Tea/Reed Cordial and active-play durations. Expand sparingly for Flow,
Stability, mastery, state resistance, Bloom or Charge handling. Avoid potion spam and
inventory-management dominance.

## 21. Social consequences

Use Mara/Oren/Neri/Daro/Lio and future inhabitants. Crossings change travel, irrigation
changes gardens, springs become gathering points, restored water enables brewing,
letters/travelers arrive, routines/trade/resources change and abandoned spaces regain
inhabitants. Avoid reducing outcomes to a thank-you line; people/world remember.

## 22. Stillfall and Lio

Preserve the premise and reveal: Lio closed the upper sluice to protect the reservoir.
Damage need not imply evil. Later conflicts involve competing water needs, displaced
habitats, survival diversions, unintended old Waykeeper interventions and historical
configurations that are no longer healthy. Early Mend repairs; later restoration
requires understanding what healthy means now. Avoid exclusively villain-driven harm.

## 23. Touch loadouts

Keep left stick, about 2–3 right actions, independent Glide and contextual TALK/PACK.
Do not add six permanent technique buttons. Develop a compact configurable subset,
active medallion with swipe/hold switching and possibly a secondary quick slot.
Knowing many techniques must not make switching cumbersome.

## 24. Weave gesture

Hold Weave → nearest compatible target gets a generous halo → drag toward another
valid target → eligible targets magnetize → live connection preview → release to
commit. Avoid tiny precision taps. Later mastery may support multiple targets,
chains, remembered links and advanced state selection.

## 25. Signature restoration

Create reusable staged physical/audiovisual presentations: establish connection;
material responds; water/structure/life moves; system reforms; water finds a path;
plants/architecture react; soundscape expands; wildlife/NPC behaviour changes; settle
into persistent recovery. Types share a recognizable Waykeeper language with unique
outcomes. Mechanically simple restoration should still be satisfying.

## 26. Visual identity

Apply porcelain, patinated bronze, restrained gold, indigo/teal textiles, riverglass,
reeds, split crescents, carved hydraulic architecture, ceramic channels, sluices,
gauges, terraced wetlands and integrated vegetation to NPCs/buildings/bridges/gear/
shrines/UI/icons/tree/VFX. Culture should visibly grow around maintaining water
relationships. Avoid generic medieval fantasy.

## 27. Opening benchmark

Before more regions, build a **10–15 minute reference-quality sequence**, not a static
grove: Mara → explore → first Mend → major recovery spectacle → reward/progression →
learn Force → Force-assisted restoration → return to a visibly changed inhabited
space. Establish final-ish touch feel, identity, human characters, dialogue, VFX,
music/audio, equipment reward, XP/tree progression, Stability/Flow UI, first node
purchase, NPC reaction, persistence and performance. First recovery is easy and
emotionally strong: "I can help this place live again."

## 28. Skills UI

Phone-readable connected tree, pan/zoom if needed. Distinguish learned, available and
unavailable nodes; show prerequisites, Skill Point price, mastery requirement and
actual behavioural effects. Distinguish general/Warden/Weaver/Living/hybrid nodes
without overload. Native metaphors: branching waterways, ceramic channels, roots,
flowing riverglass, illuminated watershed; avoid a generic sci-fi grid.

## 29. Migration

Safely extend current worlds/partial healing/unlocks/mastery/upgrades/loadout/XP/
inventory/gear/consumables/story with Stability, Flow, Skill Points, purchased nodes,
mastery prerequisites, specializations and appropriate persisted links. Translate
existing earned upgrades into equivalent learned nodes. Never delete earned progress.

## 30. Architecture

Incrementally separate player resources, abilities/definitions, nodes, mastery,
equipment modifiers, restoration conditions, world states, progression, save and UI.
Future abilities must not require rewriting region controllers. Data-drive costs,
properties, nodes/prerequisites/mastery requirements, gear and site rules. Preserve
working systems and substantial uncommitted local work. Read AGENTS.md first.

## 31. Verification

Preserve the full suite. Add Stability loss/Unmoored recovery; Flow spending,
regeneration/persistence; point acquisition/purchasing/mastery gates/tree saving;
gear/node interactions; migration of existing nodes; restoration conditions/partial
state; anti-farming; at least one cross-skill interaction; touch switching. Preserve
restoration/equipment/animation/save/multi-region coverage.

## 32. Android target

Target stable **30 FPS on a representative midrange Android phone from the last
3–4 years**, with 60 FPS possible on higher-end devices. Measure frame time, memory,
heat/battery, touch latency, loading and recovery effects. Watch transparency,
particles, vegetation, outlines, shadows, VFX and water. Triangle count is only one
factor; desktop/flagship performance is insufficient evidence.

## 33. Execution order

Keep the project playable and do not attempt every future mechanic at once.

- **A — RPG foundation:** Stability replaces hearts; Flow; both-resource HUD;
  current ability costs; forgiving Unmoored recovery.
- **B — real trees:** Skill Points; data model; migrate existing six upgrades;
  mastery gates purchases; several new behaviours/modest stats; functional touch UI.
- **C — existing journey:** rebalance Ford's distinct techniques; preserve partial
  recovery/returns; strengthen main Reedglass progression beyond Mend-only.
- **D — benchmark:** Mara/environment; signature first recovery; first tree purchase;
  polished Force lesson/use; changed inhabited return; actual phone verification.
- **E — deeper systems:** gear/node interactions and trade-offs; story-led Weave and
  initial Weaver nodes; Channel next; Bloom once living-world presentation supports it.

Do not prioritize a full Counterweight Orchard region before convincing core systems
and benchmark. Named future nodes/items above are design examples, not an instruction
to implement every one in Phase A/B.

## 34. Avoid regressions

No conventional kill-and-loot progression or primary enemy HP encounters; Mend-solving-
everything; dozens of shallow abilities; giant stat-only trees; precision gestures;
switch-puzzle restoration; button overload; rarity treadmill; invalidated gear;
discarded Warden/Weaver art; reset saves; more regions ahead of opening quality.

## 35. North star

Becoming a Waykeeper expands restoration, pushing/pulling, stability, connection,
energy, Weight/Charge, living growth, combinations, specialization and equipment.
Two players should see different possibilities at one damaged place through their
builds while both remain unmistakably Waykeepers.

Stability = grounding. Flow = technique resource. Level = broad experience.
Skill Points = choice. Mastery = disciplinary experience/eligibility.
Trees = chosen capabilities. Gear = build modification. States = world rules.
Restoration = environmental change. People = meaning.

Preserve gentleness; deepen systems; expand understanding and capabilities.
